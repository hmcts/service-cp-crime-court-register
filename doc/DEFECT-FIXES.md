# Defect-Fix Register

This service is deliberately **not** a bug-for-bug port. The thirty-four defects catalogued in the
court-register design document (§7, C1–C34) are **fixed**, and every fix is recorded here — legacy
behaviour, fixed behaviour, the test that pins the fix, and its sign-off state. The rules:

- A fix merged without a register entry is itself the defect: revert it or register it.
- The differential audit fails the build in both directions — a catalogued defect still reproduced,
  **or** a behaviour change that is not on this register. Legacy remains the oracle for everything
  not catalogued.
- Each fix is implemented and pinned by the phase that lands it; a row moves to **FIXED** only in
  the commit whose pinning test passes. Rows marked **sign-off pending** gate **cutover**, not
  implementation: a register produced for a live court centre must not carry an unapproved content
  change.
- Legacy citations are into `cpp-context-azure-legalaidagency/azure-functions/durable-functions`
  (`$DF`) at HEAD `0d63f3ae`.
- **C1–C34 are the design document's catalogue; anything numbered above it was found later** — in
  review or in the differential audit — and appended here rather than shipped as a silent
  divergence. That is the mechanism Principle I requires of an uncatalogued behaviour change:
  it earns a C-number with review, or the legacy behaviour stands.
